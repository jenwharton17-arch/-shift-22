Install Shift It

Put it on your iPhone Home Screen

Open Shift It in Safari, then:

1Tap the Share button (the square with the ↑ arrow).
2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
3Leave the name as Shift It and tap Add.
4Open Shift It from the new icon on the Home Screen.

Your shifts and other records stay on this device. Shift It updates can change the app code without intentionally replacing your saved records.
